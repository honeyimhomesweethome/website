ABOUT US

Although Honey I'm Home is a new presence on the scene, they've opened for and in Berlin and in Amsterdam, performed at 's show at the AFAS Live, and completed two UK tours.

The five-piece merges '90s alternative rock with a fresh, modern twist on their first single Wishful Thinking. The song is self-recorded and self-produced by the band and mixed by the legendary Sonny Diperri.

The song was born out of adversity—after the band's rehearsal space burned to the ground, destroying their amps, guitars, and effects. Since then, the band has been convinced they're cursed.
